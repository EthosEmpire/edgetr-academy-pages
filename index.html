Bullish and bearish patterns

Pick a shape and see how it is read, why it is not a promise, and how it fails. Every card opens a full lesson.

All patterns

Bullish patterns

Shapes conventionally read as buyers having the upper hand.

Chart pattern Bull Flag A pause after a sharp rise. Chart pattern Double Bottom Two lows at a similar level. Chart pattern Cup and Handle A rounded base, then a small pause. Chart pattern Ascending Triangle Flat resistance, rising lows. Candlestick Hammer A small body and a long lower wick after a decline. Candlestick Bullish Engulfing A larger rising candle wraps the previous falling one.
All bullish patterns

Bearish patterns

Shapes conventionally read as sellers having the upper hand.

Chart pattern Bear Flag A pause after a sharp fall. Chart pattern Double Top Two highs at a similar level. Chart pattern Head and Shoulders Three peaks, the middle one highest. Chart pattern Descending Triangle Flat support, falling highs. Candlestick Shooting Star A small body and a long upper wick after a rise. Candlestick Bearish Engulfing A larger falling candle wraps the previous rising one.
All bearish patterns

A convention, not a forecast. "Bullish" and "bearish" describe how a shape is usually interpreted. Every pattern here fails some of the time, and each lesson shows you how.
