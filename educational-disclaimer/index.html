Education, not advice

Everything on edgeTR Academy is general educational information about how markets, charts, trading tools and crypto technology work. Nothing on this site is, or should be treated as:

  • financial, investment, trading, legal, accounting or tax advice;
  • a recommendation, solicitation or offer to buy, sell, hold, long or short any asset;
  • a suggestion that any strategy, pattern, indicator or product is suitable for you;
  • a personalised recommendation of any kind. The site knows nothing about your situation.

No signals and no predictions

The Academy does not publish trade signals, alerts, price targets, asset rankings or portfolio allocations. Words such as “bullish”, “bearish”, “may indicate” or “is commonly interpreted as” describe how a concept is conventionally read. They are not forecasts.

Examples are hypothetical

Charts, numbers and scenarios on this site are illustrations built from made-up data unless a page clearly states otherwise. They are simplified to teach one idea at a time and usually ignore fees, funding, slippage, taxes and execution problems that matter in real markets.

Risk

Trading and investing involve substantial risk, including the possible loss of everything you put in and, with leverage and some derivatives, losses larger than that. Leverage, derivatives and perpetual futures can cause losses faster than many people expect, including through liquidation. Crypto assets are volatile, and mistakes with wallets, private keys, seed phrases, approvals or addresses are usually irreversible. Past performance does not indicate future results.

Accuracy

We work to keep pages accurate and we show a last-updated date on detailed pages. Markets, products, protocols and rules change, and errors are possible. Check primary sources and, where it matters, speak to a qualified professional who knows your circumstances and the rules where you live.

No relationship

Using this site does not create an advisory, fiduciary or client relationship. There are no accounts, no payments and no wallet connections on edgeTR Academy. The site will never ask you for a seed phrase, private key or password. Anyone who does, in our name or anyone else’s, is attempting a scam.

No wallet connection, accounts or trade execution

edgeTR Academy has no login, no account system and no wallet connection, and it cannot place or execute any trade. It does not hold, send or manage anyone’s funds. It is not connected to any trading system.

The short lessons use original diagrams drawn from hypothetical data, with captions. They are teaching aids, not market records.
