Exchanges, DEX & DeFi

CEXs, DEXs, AMMs, liquidity pools, lending and the risks specific to DeFi.

Start here: Centralized Exchanges (CEX)

14 pages

Centralized Exchanges (CEX) A centralized exchange is a company that runs a crypto market: it holds customers' funds, records their balances in its own ledger and matches their buy and sell orders. Beginner
Decentralized Exchanges (DEX) A decentralized exchange is a set of smart contracts on a blockchain that lets people trade tokens directly from their own wallets, with no company holding their funds. Beginner
What Is DeFi? DeFi, short for decentralized finance, is the collective name for financial services such as trading, lending and borrowing that run as smart contracts on public blockchains. Beginner
AMMs & Liquidity Pools An automated market maker (AMM) prices trades with a formula applied to a pool of two tokens, instead of matching buyers and sellers in an order book. Intermediate
Swaps & Routing A swap exchanges one token for another through liquidity pools. Routing chooses which pools to use, and slippage tolerance sets the worst result the trader accepts before the swap reverts. Intermediate
Liquidity Providers Liquidity providers deposit both tokens of a pair into a liquidity pool so that others can trade against it, and receive a share of the swap fees while carrying the pool's risks. Intermediate
Impermanent Loss Impermanent loss is the gap between the value of tokens held in a liquidity pool and the value of simply holding them, which opens whenever their relative price changes. Advanced
TVL (Total Value Locked) Total value locked (TVL) is the current market value of the assets deposited in a DeFi protocol's smart contracts. It moves with token prices as well as with deposits. Intermediate
DeFi Lending & Borrowing DeFi lending protocols pool tokens from suppliers and lend them to borrowers who post more collateral than they borrow. If the collateral's value falls too far, the loan is liquidated. Intermediate
Staking Staking means locking tokens to help secure a proof-of-stake blockchain in exchange for rewards, while accepting lock-up periods, slashing risk and changes in the token's price. Beginner
APR & APY APR is a yearly rate that leaves compounding out, and APY is a yearly rate that includes it, so the same underlying rate gives a higher APY than APR. Beginner
Smart Contract Risk Smart contract risk is the chance that the code holding funds in a blockchain application fails, is exploited or is changed, causing a loss that usually cannot be reversed. Intermediate
Counterparty Risk Counterparty risk is the risk that the other party to an arrangement, such as an exchange, a lender or a token issuer, cannot or will not do what it promised. Intermediate
Stablecoin Risks Stablecoins aim to hold a fixed value and can fail to: reserves can be weaker than claimed, redemption can be limited, market prices can de-peg and algorithmic designs can collapse. Intermediate
