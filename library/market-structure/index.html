Market Structure

Swings, trends, ranges and the transitions between them.

Start here: What Is Market Structure?

10 pages

What Is Market Structure? Market structure is the pattern of swing highs and swing lows that price leaves behind. It describes whether a market is trending up, trending down or moving sideways. Beginner
Swing Highs & Swing Lows A swing high is a peak with lower highs on both sides, and a swing low is a trough with higher lows on both sides. They are the turning points used to read market structure. Beginner
Uptrend An uptrend is a sequence of higher swing highs and higher swing lows. Price climbs in steps, and each pullback ends above the low of the one before it. Beginner▲ Bullish context
Downtrend A downtrend is a sequence of lower swing highs and lower swing lows. Price falls in steps, and each bounce ends below the high of the one before it. Beginner▼ Bearish context
Range A range is a period in which price moves sideways between an upper area and a lower area, making neither a series of higher highs and lows nor a series of lower ones. Beginner◆ Neutral
Trend Lines A trend line is a straight line drawn through successive swing lows in an uptrend or swing highs in a downtrend. It summarises the slope of a trend and marks a moving area that traders watch. Beginner
Pullbacks & Corrections A pullback is a temporary move against the prevailing trend, and a correction is a larger or longer one. Both are a normal part of trends and are only confirmed as temporary in hindsight. Beginner
Trend Transitions A trend transition is the process by which an uptrend or downtrend ends and turns into a range or the opposite trend. It usually unfolds over several swings, not in one candle. Intermediate
Break of Structure A break of structure is the moment price moves beyond a swing point that defined the trend. In an uptrend it is commonly defined as a close below the most recent higher low. Intermediate
Multi-Timeframe Structure Multi-timeframe structure is the practice of reading the same market on several chart timeframes, because one market can be in an uptrend on one timeframe and a downtrend on another. Intermediate
