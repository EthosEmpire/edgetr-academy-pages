Support & Resistance

Levels, zones, breakouts, retests, fakeouts and confluence.

Start here: Support & Resistance Basics

12 pages

Support & Resistance Basics Support and resistance are price areas where buying or selling interest has repeatedly appeared. Support sits below price, resistance sits above, and neither is an exact line or a guarantee. Beginner
Support Support is a price area below the market where buying interest has repeatedly been strong enough to pause or reverse a decline. It may hold again, and it may not. Beginner
Resistance Resistance is a price area above the market where selling interest has repeatedly been strong enough to pause or reverse an advance. It may hold again, and it may not. Beginner
Zones, Not Lines Support and resistance are better drawn as zones, bands of price, than as single lines, because markets rarely turn at exactly the same price twice. Beginner
Role Reversal Role reversal is the tendency for a broken resistance area to act later as support, and for a broken support area to act later as resistance. It is a common observation, not a rule. Intermediate
Breakouts A breakout is a move by price beyond a watched area, most often a close above a resistance zone or the edge of a range. It shows that the area gave way, not that the move continues. Beginner
Breakdowns A breakdown is a move by price below a watched support area, most often judged by a close beneath it. It shows that buyers did not hold the area this time, not that the decline continues. Beginner
Fakeouts A fakeout is a move beyond a watched support or resistance area that quickly fails, with price returning back through the level. It can only be identified after it has happened. Intermediate
Retests A retest is price returning to a level it has just broken, approaching it from the other side. It shows whether the broken area holds in its new role, and it does not always happen. Intermediate
Dynamic Support & Resistance Dynamic support and resistance are levels that move with time, such as moving averages and trend lines, in contrast to horizontal levels that stay at a fixed price. Intermediate
Confluence Confluence means several independent observations pointing to the same price area, such as a horizontal level, a trend line and a moving average meeting. It adds context, not certainty. Intermediate
Round Numbers & Psychological Levels Round numbers such as 100 or 50,000 are prices that attract attention and orders simply because they are easy to think in. Traders often watch them as possible support or resistance. Intermediate
