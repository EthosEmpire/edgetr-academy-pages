Custodial

A company, such as an exchange, holds the private keys. The user has an account and a claim on the company.

vs

Non-custodial

The user holds the private keys, usually as a seed phrase. No custodian can move the funds or block a transfer, and in a standard wallet nobody can recover a lost seed phrase. Some tokens can still be frozen by their issuer or contract.

The idea in one picture

On a blockchain, control of an asset belongs to whoever can sign with its private key. Custody is the question of who that is. In a custodial arrangement, a company holds the keys for its customers. In a non-custodial one, each user holds their own.

In plain words

Custodial is money in a bank. The bank keeps it, you have an account, and if you forget your PIN the bank can help. You are relying on the bank staying open and honest.

Non-custodial is cash in your own safe. No bank can fail with it or refuse to hand it over, though some tokens can still be frozen by their issuer. If you lose the key to the safe, there is no one to call.

When the distinction matters

What a balance really is

A balance on an exchange is an entry in the company’s records. According to its terms, the company owes the user that amount and holds assets, pooled with other customers’, to cover it. The user cannot sign anything on the blockchain. They can only ask the company to.

A balance in a non-custodial wallet is read directly from the blockchain. The wallet software is a tool for using the keys. If the software disappears, the same seed phrase restores the same funds in another wallet.

Whose failure matters

Each arrangement removes one category of risk and concentrates another.

Two bad daysHypothetical illustration

Two hypothetical people each hold 2 units of the same asset. Ana keeps hers on an exchange. Ben keeps his in a non-custodial wallet, with the seed phrase written on paper at home.

Bad day one: the exchange halts withdrawals. Ana can see her balance of 2 and cannot move it. Whether she recovers any of it depends on a process she does not control. Ben is unaffected.

Bad day two: a house fire. Ben’s phone and the paper with his seed phrase are both destroyed. His 2 units still exist on the blockchain and can never be moved again. Ana, who has also lost her phone, resets her password and logs in from a new one.

Neither arrangement avoided failure. Each was exposed to a different one.

Who can say no

A custodian can pause withdrawals, close an account or act on a legal order. This is counterparty risk. It is also what makes account recovery, and sometimes a freeze on a fraudulent transfer before it leaves the exchange, possible.

With self-custody, no custodian can block a transaction, and nobody can undo one. A token issuer or smart contract can still freeze some tokens: a stablecoin issuer, for example, can block transfers to and from an address. A transfer approved on a phishing site is as final as an intended one. The same split between a company and the user’s own keys separates the two kinds of exchange in CEX vs DEX.

Common confusion

  • Custody is not the same as hot or cold. Hot and cold describe whether keys are online. Custody describes who holds them. See Hot Wallet vs Cold Wallet.
  • An app called “wallet” may be either. Some apps hold the keys for the user and some do not. If no seed phrase or key was ever shown at setup, a third party is probably holding the keys.
  • The seed phrase is not a password. A password protects one app on one device. A seed phrase is the keys themselves, on any device, for anyone who has it.
  • “Not your keys, not your coins” is a slogan. It states the custodial risk accurately and leaves out the risk on the other side.

Security

A seed phrase gives complete control of a non-custodial wallet. No wallet provider, exchange or support agent has a reason to ask for it. A request for a seed phrase is the mark of a scam, however official it looks.

What it does NOT mean

  • Custodial does not mean insured. Protection schemes that cover bank deposits generally do not cover crypto held at an exchange, and rules vary by jurisdiction.
  • Non-custodial does not mean safe. It means that safety is entirely the user’s task. Many losses in self-custody come from lost backups and from approving malicious transactions.
  • Self-custody is not a statement of skill. It is a set of responsibilities, and it suits some circumstances and not others.
  • Neither arrangement protects the value of the asset. Custody concerns access, not price.

Key takeaway

Custodial means a third party holds the keys and the user holds a claim. Non-custodial means the user holds the keys and all of the responsibility. The first is exposed to the custodian’s failure, the second to the user’s own.
