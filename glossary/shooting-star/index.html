Glossary term

Shooting Star

Also called: shooting star candle

A candle with a small body near the bottom of its range and a long upper wick, appearing after an advance.

By convention the upper wick is at least about twice the length of the body, with little or no lower wick. It shows that price rose during the period and fell back before the close, and it is traditionally classed as a potential bearish reversal candle. The same shape after a decline is called an inverted hammer. Many shooting stars are followed by further advances.
