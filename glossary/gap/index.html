Glossary term

Gap

Also called: price gap, gap up, gap down

An empty space on a chart where a period opens at a price clearly different from the previous close, with no trading in between.

Gaps are common in markets that close overnight or at weekends, such as stocks. In crypto spot markets, which trade continuously, they are rare, though they appear on some futures charts that pause trading. A stop order can fill on the far side of a gap, away from its trigger level.
