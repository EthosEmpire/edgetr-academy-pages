Glossary term

Volatility

Also called: volatile

How much and how quickly a price moves up and down over a period.

Volatility measures the size of price changes, not their direction. It is often estimated from the standard deviation of returns or with indicators such as ATR and Bollinger Bands. Calm and turbulent periods each tend to persist for a while, but the timing of a change between them is not known in advance.
