Glossary term

Range

Also called: trading range, ranging, sideways market

A period in which price moves sideways between a rough upper boundary and a rough lower boundary.

In a range, price makes neither clear higher highs nor clear lower lows. The upper boundary acts as resistance and the lower one as support. Ranges eventually end with a breakout or a breakdown, and moves beyond a boundary frequently reverse back inside. The word also means the distance between a candle's high and low.

Learn more

Market Structure Range A range is a period in which price moves sideways between an upper area and a lower area, making neither a series of higher highs and lows nor a series of lower ones. Beginner◆ Neutral Trading Styles Range Trading Range trading is an approach built on price moving back and forth between a support area and a resistance area. It relies on the boundaries holding, and every range eventually ends. Intermediate Chart Patterns Rectangle A rectangle is a sideways pause in which price moves between two roughly horizontal boundaries. It describes balance between buyers and sellers and can resolve in either direction. Beginner
