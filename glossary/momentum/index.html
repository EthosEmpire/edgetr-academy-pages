Glossary term

Momentum

The speed and strength of a price move, often measured as the change in price over a set number of periods.

Momentum indicators such as RSI, MACD and Rate of Change turn this idea into a number. Strong momentum describes what has already happened. Momentum commonly fades before price turns, but it can also fade and then return with no reversal at all.

Learn more

Trading Styles Momentum Trading Momentum trading is an approach built on the idea that a strong, fast price move may continue for a while. Its characteristic risk is a sharp reversal once the move is exhausted. Intermediate Indicators Rate of Change (ROC) Rate of Change is a momentum indicator that shows, as a percentage, how far the current close is above or below the close a fixed number of periods ago.
