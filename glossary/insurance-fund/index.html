Glossary term

Insurance Fund

A pool of money held by a derivatives exchange to cover losses when a liquidated position loses more than the margin behind it.

The fund is built mainly from liquidation fees and from the surplus left when liquidations close at better prices than expected. It protects the winning side of trades from the losing side's shortfall. It is not insurance for an individual trader's losses, and if it runs short the exchange may resort to auto-deleveraging.
