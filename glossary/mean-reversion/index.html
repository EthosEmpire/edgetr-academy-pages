Glossary term

Mean Reversion

Also called: reversion to the mean, mean-reversion

The tendency of a price or indicator to move back toward an average after moving far away from it.

The idea underlies tools such as Bollinger Bands and the overbought and oversold zones of oscillators. It is a statistical tendency over many cases, not a rule for any single one: in a strong trend price can stay far from its average for a long time, and the average itself moves.

Learn more

Trading Styles Mean Reversion Mean reversion is an approach built on the idea that a price stretched far from its average may move back toward it. Its characteristic risk is a trend that does not revert. Intermediate
