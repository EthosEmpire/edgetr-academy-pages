Glossary term

Bullish

Also called: bull, bulls

Describing rising prices, or a view, pattern or condition associated with rising prices.

The word is applied to market conditions, chart patterns, single candles and personal opinions. A pattern labelled bullish is one conventionally associated with upward moves. The label is a naming convention, not a forecast, and such patterns often fail.

Learn more

Bull, Bear, Long & Short Bull Market & Bullish A bull market is a sustained period of generally rising prices, and bullish describes an upward condition or interpretation. Both are descriptions, not instructions to hold a long. Beginner▲ Bullish context Compare Bullish vs Bearish Bullish means expecting or describing rising prices, bearish means expecting or describing falling prices. Both are views or conditions, not positions. Beginner Bull, Bear, Long & Short Bull, Bear, Long & Short Explained Bull and bear describe a market or an opinion about it, while long and short describe a position someone holds. Keeping the two pairs apart removes a very common beginner confusion. Beginner
