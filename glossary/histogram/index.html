Glossary term

Histogram

Also called: MACD histogram

A display of values as bars; on the MACD it shows the distance between the MACD line and its signal line.

Bars above zero mean the MACD line is above the signal line, and bars below zero mean the opposite. Shrinking bars show the two lines moving closer together. More generally, a histogram is any chart of bars showing how much of something falls into each bucket, such as a volume profile.

Learn more

Indicators MACD MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence) plots the gap between a fast and a slow exponential moving average, showing whether that gap is growing or shrinking. Beginner
