Glossary term

Retest

Also called: retests, role reversal

A return of price to a level it has just broken, approaching it from the other side.

After a breakout, price often comes back to the old resistance, which may then act as support; this change is called role reversal. Not every breakout is retested, and a retest can fail, with price moving back through the level.

Learn more

Support & Resistance Retests A retest is price returning to a level it has just broken, approaching it from the other side. It shows whether the broken area holds in its new role, and it does not always happen. Intermediate Support & Resistance Role Reversal Role reversal is the tendency for a broken resistance area to act later as support, and for a broken support area to act later as resistance. It is a common observation, not a rule. Intermediate
