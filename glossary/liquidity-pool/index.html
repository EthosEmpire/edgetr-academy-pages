Glossary term

Liquidity Pool

Also called: liquidity pools

A smart contract holding reserves of two or more tokens that traders swap against on a decentralized exchange.

The tokens are deposited by liquidity providers, who earn a share of the trading fees. The size of the pool determines how much a trade moves the price. Anyone can create a pool for any token, so the existence of a pool is no indication that a token is legitimate, and liquidity can be withdrawn.
