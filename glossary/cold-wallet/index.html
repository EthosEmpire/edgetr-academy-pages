Glossary term

Cold Wallet

Also called: cold wallets, cold storage

A crypto wallet whose private keys are generated and kept offline, away from internet-connected devices.

Hardware wallets and keys recorded on paper or metal are common forms. Keeping keys offline removes most remote attack routes. It does not remove physical loss, theft or damage, a badly stored seed phrase, or the risk of approving a harmful transaction when the wallet is used.
