Glossary term

Market Cap

Also called: market capitalization, market capitalisation, mcap

The total market value of an asset, calculated as its current price multiplied by its circulating supply.

For a company it is the share price times the number of shares outstanding. It is a measure of size, not of money invested: the figure can change by a large amount on small trades, and the whole supply could not be sold at the quoted price. For crypto assets it depends on the circulating supply figure, which data providers sometimes estimate differently.

ExampleHypothetical illustration

Hypothetical: a token priced at 2 with 50 million tokens in circulation has a market cap of 100 million.

Markets & Finance Market Capitalization Market capitalization is the current price of one unit multiplied by the number of units outstanding. It measures size at the latest price, not the amount of money invested. Beginner Crypto Basics Market Cap & Fully Diluted Valuation Market cap is price multiplied by circulating supply. Fully diluted valuation (FDV) is price multiplied by max or total supply, showing the size implied if every unit existed today. Intermediate Compare Market Cap vs Fully Diluted Valuation Market cap is price times the supply circulating today. Fully diluted valuation (FDV) is price times the maximum supply, which assumes today's price would hold for every future unit. Intermediate
