Glossary term

Triangle Pattern

Also called: ascending triangle, descending triangle, symmetrical triangle

A chart pattern in which price swings narrow between two converging trend lines.

An ascending triangle has a flat top and rising lows, a descending triangle has a flat bottom and falling highs, and in a symmetrical triangle both lines converge. Textbooks associate each type with a typical direction of breakout, but in practice triangles break either way, and false breaks near the tip are common.

Learn more

Chart Patterns Symmetrical Triangle A symmetrical triangle is a series of lower highs and higher lows that converge toward a point. It shows a market in shrinking balance and has no built-in direction. Beginner Chart Patterns Ascending Triangle An ascending triangle is a flat line of highs above a rising line of lows. It shows buyers paying more on each dip while sellers hold one level, and is conventionally read as bullish. Beginner▲ Bullish context Chart Patterns Descending Triangle A descending triangle is a flat line of lows beneath a falling line of highs. It shows sellers accepting less on each rally while buyers hold one level, and is conventionally read as bearish. Beginner▼ Bearish context
