Glossary term

Fakeout

Also called: false breakout, failed breakout, bull trap, bear trap

A move beyond a support or resistance level that quickly reverses back inside the prior range.

Fakeouts are common, which is why a break of a level is not treated as confirmed just because price traded through it. A failed move above resistance is sometimes called a bull trap, and a failed move below support a bear trap. Whether a move was a fakeout is only known afterwards.
