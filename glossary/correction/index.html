Glossary term

Correction

Also called: market correction

A decline that retraces part of a previous advance, usually larger or longer than a pullback.

In stock markets a correction is commonly defined as a fall of 10% or more from a recent high, short of the 20% often used for a bear market. In crypto, where volatility is higher, the word is used more loosely. A correction can also unfold sideways over time instead of through a fall in price.

Learn more

Market Structure Pullbacks & Corrections A pullback is a temporary move against the prevailing trend, and a correction is a larger or longer one. Both are a normal part of trends and are only confirmed as temporary in hindsight. Beginner
