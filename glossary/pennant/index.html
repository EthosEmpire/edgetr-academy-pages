Glossary term

Pennant

Also called: pennant pattern

A chart pattern in which a sharp move is followed by a small symmetrical triangle of narrowing price swings.

Like a flag, a pennant follows a flagpole and is conventionally classed as a short-term continuation pattern. The difference is the shape of the pause: converging lines for a pennant, parallel lines for a flag. It is smaller and shorter-lived than a triangle, and it can break in either direction.
