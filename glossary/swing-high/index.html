Glossary term

Swing High

Also called: swing highs

A peak on a chart: a high with lower highs on both sides of it.

Swing highs mark where a rise paused or turned. They are the reference points used to define trends and to draw resistance levels and trend lines. A swing high can only be identified after later candles have formed, and how many candles are required on each side is a matter of convention.

Learn more

Market Structure Swing Highs & Swing Lows A swing high is a peak with lower highs on both sides, and a swing low is a trough with higher lows on both sides. They are the turning points used to read market structure. Beginner
