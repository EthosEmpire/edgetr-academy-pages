Glossary term

Ether

Also called: ETH

The native currency of the Ethereum network, used to pay transaction fees and staked by validators.

Users pay gas fees in ether, and validators lock it up as stake to take part in consensus. Its smallest unit is the wei, and gas prices are usually quoted in gwei, one billionth of an ether. Ethereum is the network and ether is the currency, though the two names are often used interchangeably.

Crypto Basics Ethereum: An Overview Ethereum is a public blockchain, launched in 2015, that runs programs called smart contracts. Its native asset is ether (ETH), which normally pays for each transaction on the network. Beginner Crypto Basics Gas & Network Fees A network fee is what a blockchain charges to process a transaction. On Ethereum-style chains it is measured in gas: the fee is the gas used multiplied by the price per unit of gas. Beginner
