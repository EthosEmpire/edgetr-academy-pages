Glossary term

FOMO

Also called: fear of missing out

Fear of missing out: the anxiety of watching a price rise without being in the trade, and the urge to buy because others appear to be profiting.

It tends to be strongest after a large move has already happened. Decisions made under FOMO typically skip the usual planning of entry, exit and size. It is a normal emotional reaction, not a market indicator, and it is deliberately provoked by promoters and scammers.

Trading Psychology FOMO (Fear of Missing Out) FOMO is the anxious urge to join a price move because others appear to be profiting from it, usually after much of the move has already happened. Beginner Trading Psychology Herd Behavior Herd behavior is the tendency of people to copy what a large group is doing, buying because others buy and selling because others sell, instead of judging independently.
