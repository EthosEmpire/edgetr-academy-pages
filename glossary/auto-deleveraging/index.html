Glossary term

Auto-Deleveraging

Also called: ADL, auto deleveraging, auto-deleverage

A last-resort mechanism in which a derivatives exchange closes part of profitable traders' positions to cover liquidation losses it cannot otherwise absorb.

It is used when a liquidated position cannot be closed in the market at a price that covers its losses and the insurance fund is not sufficient. Traders on the opposite side are typically ranked by profit and leverage, and those at the top have positions reduced at a set price. It is rare, but it means a profitable position can be closed without its holder choosing to. Rules differ between exchanges.

Derivatives & Perpetuals Liquidation on Perpetuals Liquidation on perpetuals is the forced closing of a leveraged position by the exchange when its margin balance, measured at the mark price, falls to the maintenance margin. Intermediate Derivatives & Perpetuals Liquidation Cascades A liquidation cascade is a chain reaction in which forced closures of leveraged positions push the price further, which triggers more forced closures.
