Proof of Stake

Also called: proof-of-stake, PoS

A consensus method in which validators lock up coins as stake and are chosen to propose and confirm blocks.

Validators earn rewards for honest work and can lose stake for provable misbehaviour. It uses far less energy than proof of work, and Ethereum has used it since 2022. Common criticisms are that large holders gain more influence and that the designs are more complex.

Learn more

Blockchain Proof of Stake Proof of stake secures a blockchain with locked capital: validators deposit the network's coin as a stake, are chosen to propose and vote on blocks, and can lose that stake for misbehaving. Intermediate Compare Proof of Work vs Proof of Stake Proof of Work secures a blockchain with computation and energy. Proof of Stake secures it with locked-up coins. Both make it costly to rewrite the ledger. Intermediate
