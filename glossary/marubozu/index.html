Glossary term

Marubozu

Also called: marubozu candle

A candle with a long body and little or no wick at either end.

Price opened at or near one extreme of the period and closed at or near the other, moving in one direction for most of the period. A bullish marubozu closes at its high and a bearish one at its low. It shows strong one-sided activity in that period and does not indicate that the move continues.

Learn more

Candlesticks Marubozu A Marubozu is a candle that is all body, with little or no wick at either end. One side controlled the period from the open to the close. Beginner
