Glossary term

Flag Pattern

Also called: bull flag, bear flag, flagpole

A chart pattern in which a sharp move is followed by a short, narrow channel that slopes against that move.

The sharp move is called the flagpole. A bull flag slopes gently down after a rise, and a bear flag slopes gently up after a fall. It is conventionally classed as a continuation pattern, though price can also leave the flag in the opposite direction.

Learn more

Chart Patterns Bull Flag A bull flag is a sharp rise followed by a short, tight drift downward between two roughly parallel lines. It is conventionally read as a pause in an advance, and it often fails. Beginner▲ Bullish context Chart Patterns Bear Flag A bear flag is a sharp fall followed by a short, tight drift upward between two roughly parallel lines. It is conventionally read as a pause in a decline, and it often resolves the other way. Beginner▼ Bearish context
