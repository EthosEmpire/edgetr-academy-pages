Glossary term

Double Top

Also called: double top pattern

A chart pattern with two peaks at a similar level separated by a trough, forming a shape like the letter M.

It is traditionally classed as a bearish reversal pattern and is conventionally treated as complete only when price closes below the trough between the peaks, sometimes called the neckline. Until then it is simply two tests of resistance, and many apparent double tops resolve upward.
