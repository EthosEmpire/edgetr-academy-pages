Glossary term

Bearish

Also called: bear, bears

Describing falling prices, or a view, pattern or condition associated with falling prices.

The word is applied to market conditions, chart patterns, single candles and personal opinions. A pattern labelled bearish is one conventionally associated with downward moves. The label is a naming convention, not a forecast, and such patterns often fail.

Learn more

Bull, Bear, Long & Short Bear Market & Bearish A bear market is a sustained period of generally falling prices, and bearish describes a downward condition or interpretation. Both are descriptions, not instructions to open a short. Beginner▼ Bearish context Compare Bullish vs Bearish Bullish means expecting or describing rising prices, bearish means expecting or describing falling prices. Both are views or conditions, not positions. Beginner Bull, Bear, Long & Short Bull, Bear, Long & Short Explained Bull and bear describe a market or an opinion about it, while long and short describe a position someone holds. Keeping the two pairs apart removes a very common beginner confusion. Beginner
