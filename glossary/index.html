248 terms

A-Z Glossary

Short, plain-language definitions of trading and crypto terms. Each one links to a deeper page when there is one.

248 terms

ABCDEFGHIKLMNOPRSTUVWY

A

Addressalso: wallet address, public address
A string of letters and numbers, derived from a public key, that identifies where crypto can be sent on a blockchain.
ADXalso: Average Directional Index
An indicator on a 0-100 scale that measures how strong a trend is, without showing its direction.
Airdropalso: airdrops
A distribution of tokens sent to many wallet addresses at no charge, usually to reward early users or publicise a project.
Altcoinalso: altcoins, alts
Any cryptocurrency other than bitcoin.
AMMalso: automated market maker, automated market makers
An automated market maker: a smart contract that prices trades with a mathematical formula applied to a pool of tokens, instead of matching orders.
APRalso: annual percentage rate
Annual percentage rate: a yearly rate of interest or reward that does not include the effect of compounding.
APYalso: annual percentage yield
Annual percentage yield: a yearly rate of return that includes the effect of compounding.
Arbitragealso: arbitrageur, arbitrageurs
Buying an asset in one market and selling it in another at nearly the same time to capture a difference in price.
Askalso: ask price, offer, offer price
The lowest price at which a seller is currently willing to sell, also called the offer.
Assetalso: assets, asset class
Anything of value that can be owned and traded, such as a stock, bond, commodity, currency or cryptocurrency.
ATHalso: all-time high
The highest price an asset has ever traded at, short for all-time high.
ATLalso: all-time low
The lowest price an asset has ever traded at, short for all-time low.
ATRalso: Average True Range
A volatility indicator that averages the true range of recent periods, showing how much price typically moves per candle.
Auto-Deleveragingalso: ADL, auto deleveraging, auto-deleverage
A last-resort mechanism in which a derivatives exchange closes part of profitable traders' positions to cover liquidation losses it cannot otherwise absorb.

B

Backtestingalso: backtest, backtested
Testing a set of trading rules on historical price data to see how they would have performed.
Backwardation
A market condition in which futures prices are lower than the spot price, and later expiries are priced below nearer ones.
Basisalso: futures basis, futures premium
The difference between the price of a futures contract and the spot price of the same asset.
Bear Market
A prolonged period in which prices in a market are generally falling.
Bearishalso: bear, bears
Describing falling prices, or a view, pattern or condition associated with falling prices.
Bidalso: bid price
The highest price at which a buyer is currently willing to buy.
Bid-Ask Spreadalso: bid/ask spread, bid-offer spread
The difference between the highest price a buyer is offering (the bid) and the lowest price a seller is asking (the ask).
Bitcoinalso: BTC
The first cryptocurrency, a decentralized digital money whose supply is limited to about 21 million coins.
Blockalso: blocks, block height
A batch of transactions added to a blockchain as a single unit, together with a reference to the previous block.
Block Rewardalso: block rewards, block subsidy
The payment a miner or validator receives for adding a block, made up of newly created coins plus transaction fees.
Blockchainalso: blockchains, distributed ledger
A shared record of transactions grouped into blocks, where each block is cryptographically linked to the one before it.
Bodyalso: real body, candle body
The thick part of a candlestick, spanning the opening and closing prices of the period.
Bollinger Bandsalso: Bollinger Band, BB
A volatility indicator made of a moving average with one band above and one below it, set a number of standard deviations away.
Bondalso: bonds
A loan made by an investor to a government or company, which pays interest and returns the principal at a set date.
Breakdownalso: break down
A move in which price falls below a support level or out of the bottom of a chart pattern.
Breakoutalso: break out
A move in which price pushes above a resistance level or out of a chart pattern.
Bridgealso: cross-chain bridge, bridging, wrapped token
A system that moves assets or data between two separate blockchains.
Brokeralso: brokers, brokerage
A firm or person that places trades in a market on behalf of clients.
Bull Marketalso: bull run
A prolonged period in which prices in a market are generally rising.
Bullishalso: bull, bulls
Describing rising prices, or a view, pattern or condition associated with rising prices.

C

Call Optionalso: call options
An option that gives its buyer the right to buy the underlying asset at the strike price.
Candlestickalso: candle, candlestick chart, Japanese candlestick
A chart symbol that shows the open, high, low and close of one time period as a thick body with thin lines above and below.
Capitulation
A phase of intense selling in which many holders give up and sell at a loss, usually on high volume after a long decline.
CEXalso: centralized exchange, centralized exchanges, centralised exchange
A centralized exchange: a company that runs a trading platform and holds customers' funds in its own custody.
Channelalso: price channel, trend channel, ascending channel, descending channel
Two roughly parallel trend lines that contain price between them as it trends or moves sideways.
Circulating Supply
The number of coins or tokens that are currently available to the public and able to trade.
Coinalso: coins, native coin
A cryptocurrency that is the native asset of its own blockchain, such as bitcoin on Bitcoin or ether on Ethereum.
Cold Walletalso: cold wallets, cold storage
A crypto wallet whose private keys are generated and kept offline, away from internet-connected devices.
Collateralalso: overcollateralized
Assets pledged as security for a loan or a leveraged position.
Commodityalso: commodities
A basic physical good that is interchangeable with other units of the same kind, such as gold, oil or wheat.
Confirmationalso: confirmations, confirmed transaction
The inclusion of a transaction in a block; each later block added on top counts as one more confirmation.
Confluence
The situation where several independent tools or observations point to the same price area or conclusion.
Consensusalso: consensus mechanism, consensus algorithm
The process by which the computers in a blockchain network agree on which transactions are valid and in what order.
Consolidationalso: consolidating
A pause in which price moves sideways in a relatively narrow area after a directional move.
Contango
A market condition in which futures prices are higher than the spot price, and later expiries are priced above nearer ones.
Correctionalso: market correction
A decline that retraces part of a previous advance, usually larger or longer than a pullback.
Cross Margin
A margin mode in which the whole account balance is shared as margin across all open positions.
Cryptocurrencyalso: cryptocurrencies, crypto asset, crypto assets
A digital asset whose ownership and transfers are recorded on a blockchain and secured by cryptography instead of by a central institution.
Cup and Handlealso: cup-and-handle, cup with handle
A chart pattern made of a rounded, U-shaped decline and recovery (the cup) followed by a smaller pullback (the handle).
Custodialalso: custodial wallet, custodial wallets
Describing a wallet or service in which a third party, such as an exchange, holds the private keys on the user's behalf.

D

Day Tradingalso: day trader, intraday trading
A trading style in which positions are opened and closed within the same day, with none held overnight.
Death Cross
A moving average crossover in which a shorter average, typically the 50-period, falls below a longer one, typically the 200-period.
DeFialso: decentralized finance, decentralised finance
Decentralized finance: financial services such as trading, lending and borrowing run by smart contracts on a blockchain instead of by companies.
Derivativealso: derivatives, underlying asset
A contract whose value is based on the price of something else, called the underlying asset.
DEXalso: decentralized exchange, decentralized exchanges, decentralised exchange
A decentralized exchange: a set of smart contracts that lets users trade tokens directly from their own wallets.
Divergencealso: bullish divergence, bearish divergence
A disagreement between price and an indicator, for example price making a higher high while the indicator makes a lower high.
Dojialso: doji candle
A candle whose open and close are almost the same price, often read as indecision.
Double Bottomalso: double bottom pattern
A chart pattern with two troughs at a similar level separated by a peak, forming a shape like the letter W.
Double Topalso: double top pattern
A chart pattern with two peaks at a similar level separated by a trough, forming a shape like the letter M.
Downtrendalso: down trend
A series of lower highs and lower lows, showing that price has been falling over the period viewed.
Drawdownalso: max drawdown, maximum drawdown
The decline in an account or asset from a peak to a later low, usually given as a percentage.
DYORalso: do your own research
Do your own research: a reminder to check information independently before acting on someone else's claim.

E

EMAalso: Exponential Moving Average
A moving average that gives more weight to recent prices, so it reacts faster than a simple moving average.
Emissionsalso: token emissions, emission schedule
New tokens created and distributed over time as rewards, for example to miners, validators, stakers or liquidity providers.
Engulfing Patternalso: engulfing, engulfing candle, bullish engulfing, bearish engulfing
A two-candle pattern in which the second candle's body completely covers the body of the first, in the opposite direction.
Entryalso: entry price
The price and moment at which a position is opened.
ETFalso: ETFs, exchange-traded fund
A fund that holds a basket of assets and whose shares trade on a stock exchange like an ordinary stock.
Etheralso: ETH
The native currency of the Ethereum network, used to pay transaction fees and staked by validators.
Ethereum
A public blockchain that runs smart contracts, allowing programs and tokens to operate on a shared network.
Euphoria
A phase of extreme optimism in which prices rise quickly and risks are widely ignored.
Evening Staralso: evening star pattern
A three-candle pattern after an advance: a long up candle, a small-bodied candle, then a long down candle.
Exchangealso: exchanges, trading venue
A marketplace, physical or electronic, where buyers and sellers trade assets under a common set of rules.
Exitalso: exit price
The price and moment at which a position is closed, whether at a profit or at a loss.
Expectancyalso: expected value per trade
The average amount a trading method has gained or lost per trade, combining win rate with the average size of wins and losses.

F

Fakeoutalso: false breakout, failed breakout, bull trap, bear trap
A move beyond a support or resistance level that quickly reverses back inside the prior range.
FDValso: fully diluted valuation, fully diluted market cap
Fully diluted valuation: an asset's current price multiplied by its maximum supply.
Fillalso: filled, partial fill, execution
The execution of an order, in whole or in part.
Finalityalso: transaction finality
The point at which a blockchain transaction can no longer be reversed or altered.
Flag Patternalso: bull flag, bear flag, flagpole
A chart pattern in which a sharp move is followed by a short, narrow channel that slopes against that move.
FOMOalso: fear of missing out
Fear of missing out: the anxiety of watching a price rise without being in the trade, and the urge to buy because others appear to be profiting.
Forexalso: FX, foreign exchange, currency pair
The global market in which national currencies are exchanged for one another.
Forkalso: hard fork, soft fork
A change to a blockchain's rules, or a split of one chain into two that share the same history up to that point.
FUDalso: fear, uncertainty and doubt
Fear, uncertainty and doubt: negative information or rumour, accurate or not, that spreads worry about an asset or project.
Fundamental Analysisalso: FA
The study of an asset's underlying economic value, using factors such as earnings, usage, supply and the wider economy.
Funding Ratealso: funding rates, funding fee, funding payment
A periodic payment exchanged between long and short holders of a perpetual futures contract to keep its price near the spot price.
Futures Contractalso: futures, futures contracts, dated futures
A standardised agreement to buy or sell an asset at a set price on a set future date.

G

Gapalso: price gap, gap up, gap down
An empty space on a chart where a period opens at a price clearly different from the previous close, with no trading in between.
Gasalso: gas limit, gas price, gwei
The unit that measures how much computational work a transaction requires on Ethereum and similar blockchains.
Gas Feealso: gas fees, network fee, network fees, transaction fee
The fee paid to a blockchain network to process a transaction, normally charged in the network's native coin.
Golden Cross
A moving average crossover in which a shorter average, typically the 50-period, rises above a longer one, typically the 200-period.
Governance Tokenalso: governance tokens
A token that gives its holders the right to vote on decisions about a protocol, such as upgrades, fees or use of the treasury.

H

Halvingalso: Bitcoin halving, halvening
A scheduled event that cuts in half the number of new bitcoins created with each block.
Hammeralso: hammer candle
A candle with a small body near the top of its range and a long lower wick, appearing after a decline.
Haramialso: bullish harami, bearish harami
A two-candle pattern in which a small second body sits entirely inside the body of the larger candle before it.
Hardware Walletalso: hardware wallets
A small dedicated device that stores private keys offline and signs transactions internally, so the keys never leave the device.
Hashalso: hash function, hashing, hash rate
A fixed-length string of characters produced by running data through a mathematical function, acting as a digital fingerprint of that data.
Head and Shouldersalso: head-and-shoulders, inverse head and shoulders, H&S
A chart pattern of three peaks, with the middle peak (the head) higher than the two on either side (the shoulders).
Health Factor
A number used by DeFi lending protocols to show how safely a loan is collateralized; below 1, the position can be liquidated.
Hedgealso: hedging, hedged
A position opened to reduce the risk of another position by gaining when the first one loses.
Higher Highalso: higher highs, HH
A swing high that is above the previous swing high.
Higher Lowalso: higher lows, HL
A swing low that is above the previous swing low.
Histogramalso: MACD histogram
A display of values as bars; on the MACD it shows the distance between the MACD line and its signal line.
HODLalso: hodling, hodler
Crypto slang for holding an asset for the long term regardless of price swings.
Honeypotalso: honeypot token, honeypot scam
A scam token whose smart contract lets people buy it but prevents them from selling.
Hot Walletalso: hot wallets
A crypto wallet whose private keys are kept on a device connected to the internet, such as a phone app or browser extension.

I

Impermanent Lossalso: divergence loss
The shortfall a liquidity provider has compared with simply holding the same tokens, caused by the pooled tokens' prices moving relative to each other.
Indexalso: market index, indexes, indices
A calculated number that tracks the combined price of a defined group of assets, such as the largest companies in a country.
Index Pricealso: spot index price
A reference price for an asset, calculated as an average of its spot price across several exchanges.
Indicatoralso: technical indicator, indicators
A calculation based on price, volume or both, drawn on a chart to summarise some aspect of market behaviour.
Inflationalso: token inflation
A general rise in the prices of goods and services over time, which reduces what each unit of money can buy.
Initial Margin
The minimum amount of collateral required to open a leveraged position.
Institutional Traderalso: institutional traders, institutional investor, institutional investors
A professional organisation that trades large sums, such as a fund, bank, pension scheme or trading firm.
Insurance Fund
A pool of money held by a derivatives exchange to cover losses when a liquidated position loses more than the margin behind it.
Interest Ratealso: interest rates
The cost of borrowing money, or the reward for lending it, expressed as a percentage of the amount per year.
Isolated Margin
A margin mode in which each position has its own separate margin, so its liquidation normally consumes only that amount.

K

KYCalso: Know Your Customer, identity verification
Know Your Customer: the identity checks that financial companies, including most centralized crypto exchanges, are required to carry out on their users.

L

Layer 1also: layer-1, L1, base layer
A base blockchain that processes and finalises transactions on its own network, such as Bitcoin or Ethereum.
Layer 2also: layer-2, L2
A network built on top of a base blockchain that processes transactions off the main chain while relying on it for security.
Lendingalso: borrowing, DeFi lending, lending protocol
Supplying assets to borrowers in return for interest; in DeFi this is done through smart contracts that pool deposits and lend against collateral.
Leveragealso: leveraged
Using borrowed funds or margin to control a position larger than your own capital, which multiplies both gains and losses.
Limit Orderalso: limit orders
An order to buy or sell at a specified price or better, which waits in the order book until it can be filled.
Liquidationalso: liquidated, liquidations
The forced closing of a leveraged position by the exchange or protocol when its margin no longer covers its losses.
Liquidation Cascadealso: liquidation cascades, cascading liquidations
A chain reaction in which forced closures of leveraged positions push the price further, triggering more liquidations.
Liquidation Pricealso: liq price
The price at which a leveraged position's margin falls to the maintenance requirement and liquidation begins.
Liquidityalso: liquid market, illiquid
How easily an asset can be bought or sold in size without moving its price much.
Liquidity Poolalso: liquidity pools
A smart contract holding reserves of two or more tokens that traders swap against on a decentralized exchange.
Liquidity Provideralso: liquidity providers, LP, LP token
Someone who deposits tokens into a liquidity pool so that others can trade against them, in return for a share of trading fees.
Longalso: long position, going long
A position that gains value when the price rises and loses value when it falls.
Long/Short Ratioalso: long-short ratio, long/short ratios, L/S ratio
A statistic comparing the number of accounts, or the size of positions, that are long with those that are short on a derivatives exchange.
Lower Highalso: lower highs, LH
A swing high that is below the previous swing high.
Lower Lowalso: lower lows, LL
A swing low that is below the previous swing low.

M

MACDalso: Moving Average Convergence Divergence
A momentum indicator built from the difference between two exponential moving averages of price, usually 12 and 26 periods.
Maintenance Margin
The minimum collateral that must remain in a leveraged position to keep it open.
Makeralso: maker order, maker fee
A trader whose order rests in the order book and adds liquidity until someone else trades against it.
Marginalso: margin trading
The funds a trader sets aside as security to open and keep a leveraged position.
Margin Call
A notice that an account's margin has fallen too low and that more funds must be added or positions reduced.
Mark Price
A reference price calculated by a derivatives exchange and used to value open positions and decide liquidations.
Marketalso: markets
Any place or system where buyers and sellers meet to exchange assets and where prices are formed.
Market Capalso: market capitalization, market capitalisation, mcap
The total market value of an asset, calculated as its current price multiplied by its circulating supply.
Market Makeralso: market makers, market making
A firm or trader that continuously quotes both a buy price and a sell price, providing liquidity to other participants.
Market Orderalso: market orders
An order to buy or sell immediately at the best prices currently available.
Marubozualso: marubozu candle
A candle with a long body and little or no wick at either end.
Max Supplyalso: maximum supply, supply cap
The largest number of coins or tokens that can ever exist under an asset's rules.
Mean Reversionalso: reversion to the mean, mean-reversion
The tendency of a price or indicator to move back toward an average after moving far away from it.
Meme Coinalso: meme coins, memecoin, memecoins
A cryptocurrency based on an internet joke, image or cultural trend, with little or no intended function beyond that.
Mempoolalso: memory pool
The waiting area where valid transactions sit after being broadcast and before being included in a block.
Mineralso: miners, mining pool
A participant in a proof-of-work network that uses computing power to compete for the right to add the next block.
Miningalso: crypto mining, bitcoin mining, mining difficulty
The process of using computing power to add new blocks to a proof-of-work blockchain in return for newly issued coins and transaction fees.
Momentum
The speed and strength of a price move, often measured as the change in price over a set number of periods.
Morning Staralso: morning star pattern
A three-candle pattern after a decline: a long down candle, a small-bodied candle, then a long up candle.
Moving Averagealso: moving averages, MA
The average price over a set number of recent periods, recalculated with each new candle and drawn as a line on the chart.

N

Nodealso: nodes, full node
A computer that runs a blockchain's software, keeps a copy of its data and passes transactions and blocks to other nodes.
Non-Custodialalso: non-custodial wallet, noncustodial, self-custody, self-custodial
Describing a wallet in which the user alone holds the private keys, with no custodian able to move or block the funds, although some tokens can still be frozen by their issuer.

O

OBValso: On-Balance Volume, on balance volume
A cumulative volume indicator that adds a period's volume when price closes higher and subtracts it when price closes lower.
OHLCalso: OHLCV, open-high-low-close
The four prices that summarise a period of trading: open, high, low and close.
Open Interestalso: OI
The total number of derivative contracts that are currently open and have not been closed or settled.
Optionalso: options, options contract, premium, strike price, expiry
A contract that gives its buyer the right, but not the obligation, to buy or sell an asset at a fixed price up to or on a set date.
Order Bookalso: orderbook, order-book
The live list of all open buy and sell orders for a market, arranged by price.
Oscillatoralso: oscillators
An indicator that moves back and forth within a fixed range or around a centre line, used mainly to gauge momentum.
Overbought
An indicator convention for a reading in the upper zone of an oscillator's scale, such as RSI above 70.
Overfittingalso: curve fitting, over-optimization
Tuning a trading method so closely to past data that it captures random noise and fails on new data.
Oversold
An indicator convention for a reading in the lower zone of an oscillator's scale, such as RSI below 30.

P

Pennantalso: pennant pattern
A chart pattern in which a sharp move is followed by a small symmetrical triangle of narrowing price swings.
Perpetual Futuresalso: perpetual future, perpetual contract, perpetual swap, perpetuals, perps
A futures contract with no expiry date, kept close to the spot price by regular funding payments between longs and shorts.
Phishingalso: phishing attack, phishing site
A scam in which an attacker poses as a trusted company, site or person to trick someone into revealing secrets or approving a harmful action.
PnLalso: P&L, PNL, profit and loss
Profit and loss: the money gained or lost on a position or over a period.
Point of Controlalso: POC
The price level with the highest traded volume in a volume profile.
Positionalso: open position
The amount of an asset or contract a trader currently holds, either long or short.
Position Sizingalso: position size
Deciding how large a position to open, usually from the amount of money that would be lost if the stop loss is hit.
Position Tradingalso: position trader
A trading style that holds positions for weeks, months or longer, following large trends.
Price Impact
The change in a market's or pool's price caused by a trade itself, which grows with the size of the trade relative to available liquidity.
Private Keyalso: private keys
A secret number that proves ownership of crypto at an address and is used to sign transactions that spend it.
Proof of Stakealso: proof-of-stake, PoS
A consensus method in which validators lock up coins as stake and are chosen to propose and confirm blocks.
Proof of Workalso: proof-of-work, PoW
A consensus method in which miners compete to solve a computational puzzle, and the winner adds the next block.
Public Keyalso: public keys
A cryptographic value derived from a private key that lets others verify signatures and from which addresses are generated.
Pullbackalso: retracement, dip
A temporary move against the prevailing trend, after which the trend may or may not resume.
Pump and Dumpalso: pump-and-dump, pump and dump scheme, P&D
A scheme in which organisers buy an asset, promote it to drive the price up, then sell to the buyers they attracted.
Put Optionalso: put options
An option that gives its buyer the right to sell the underlying asset at the strike price.

R

Rangealso: trading range, ranging, sideways market
A period in which price moves sideways between a rough upper boundary and a rough lower boundary.
Realized PnLalso: realized P&L, realised PnL, realized profit and loss
The profit or loss that has been locked in by closing a position or part of it.
Resistancealso: resistance level, resistance zone
A price area where selling has previously been strong enough to stop or slow a rise.
Retail Traderalso: retail traders, retail investor, retail investors
An individual who trades with personal money, as opposed to a professional trading on behalf of an institution.
Retestalso: retests, role reversal
A return of price to a level it has just broken, approaching it from the other side.
Risk of Ruin
The probability that a series of losses reduces an account to the point where trading can no longer continue.
Risk/Reward Ratioalso: risk-reward ratio, risk/reward, reward-to-risk ratio, R:R
A comparison of the amount a trade plans to risk with the amount it aims to gain.
Rollupalso: rollups, optimistic rollup, ZK rollup, zk-rollup
A type of layer 2 that executes transactions off the main chain and posts compressed data or proofs back to it in batches.
RSIalso: Relative Strength Index
A momentum indicator on a 0-100 scale that compares recent gains with recent losses.
Rug Pullalso: rug pulls, rugpull, exit scam
A scam in which a project's creators attract buyers and then take the funds, typically by withdrawing the liquidity or selling their holdings.

S

Satoshialso: sat, sats, satoshis
The smallest unit of bitcoin: one hundred millionth of a bitcoin (0.00000001 BTC).
Scalpingalso: scalper
A trading style that aims to capture very small price moves over seconds or minutes, many times a day.
Seed Phrasealso: seed phrases, recovery phrase, secret recovery phrase, mnemonic phrase
A list of usually 12 or 24 words that encodes the master secret from which all of a wallet's private keys are derived.
Shooting Staralso: shooting star candle
A candle with a small body near the bottom of its range and a long upper wick, appearing after an advance.
Shortalso: short position, going short, short selling
A position that gains value when the price falls and loses value when it rises.
Short Squeezealso: short squeezes
A rapid price rise that forces holders of short positions to buy back, which adds to the buying and pushes the price higher still.
Signal Line
A moving average of an indicator's own values, plotted alongside it to make its turns easier to see.
Signaturealso: digital signature, signing
Cryptographic proof, created with a private key, that the key's owner authorised a specific transaction or message.
Slashingalso: slashed
A penalty in proof-of-stake networks that destroys part of a validator's staked coins for provable misbehaviour.
Slippagealso: slippage tolerance
The difference between the price expected when an order is sent and the price at which it actually fills.
SMAalso: Simple Moving Average
The plain average of closing prices over a set number of periods, with every period weighted equally.
Smart Contractalso: smart contracts
A program stored on a blockchain that runs automatically, exactly as written, when it is called by a transaction.
Spinning Topalso: spinning top candle
A candle with a small body and upper and lower wicks of similar, moderate length.
Spotalso: spot market, spot trading, spot price
The market in which assets are bought and sold for immediate delivery at the current price.
Spread
The difference between two prices, most often the gap between the best bid and the best ask.
Stablecoinalso: stablecoins
A crypto token designed to hold a steady value, usually one US dollar.
Stakingalso: staking rewards
Locking up coins in a proof-of-stake network to help validate transactions, in return for rewards.
Stochastic Oscillatoralso: Stochastic, stochastics, %K, %D
A momentum indicator on a 0-100 scale that shows where the latest close sits within the high-low range of recent periods.
Stockalso: stocks, share, shares, equity
A unit of ownership in a company, also called a share or equity.
Stop Lossalso: stop-loss, stop-loss order, SL
An order placed in advance to close a position if price moves against it to a chosen level, limiting the planned loss.
Stop Orderalso: stop orders, stop-market order, stop-limit order
An order that stays inactive until price reaches a chosen trigger level, then becomes a market or limit order.
Supportalso: support level, support zone
A price area where buying has previously been strong enough to stop or slow a decline.
Swapalso: token swap, swaps
An exchange of one crypto token for another, typically made directly against a liquidity pool on a decentralized exchange.
Swing Highalso: swing highs
A peak on a chart: a high with lower highs on both sides of it.
Swing Lowalso: swing lows
A trough on a chart: a low with higher lows on both sides of it.
Swing Tradingalso: swing trader
A trading style that holds positions for several days to a few weeks to capture individual price swings.

T

Take Profitalso: take-profit, take-profit order, TP
An order placed in advance to close a position at a chosen price in the profitable direction.
Takeralso: taker order, taker fee
A trader whose order fills immediately against orders already resting in the order book, removing liquidity.
Technical Analysisalso: TA, chart analysis
The study of price charts, volume and indicators to describe market behaviour and form views about possible future moves.
Timeframealso: time frame, chart interval
The length of time each candle or bar on a chart represents, such as one hour or one day.
Tokenalso: tokens, ERC-20
A crypto asset created by a smart contract on an existing blockchain, instead of being that blockchain's native coin.
Token Approvalalso: token approvals, token allowance, unlimited approval
Permission given to a smart contract to move a set amount of a token from the user's wallet.
Token Unlockalso: token unlocks, vesting schedule, cliff unlock
A scheduled release of tokens that were previously locked, usually those allocated to a project's team, investors or treasury.
Tokenomicsalso: token economics
The economic design of a crypto token: its supply, distribution, issuance schedule and uses.
Total Supply
The number of coins or tokens that exist right now, including locked or reserved ones, minus any that have been burned.
Trendalso: trending
The general direction in which a price is moving over a chosen period: up, down or sideways.
Trend Linealso: trendline, trend lines, trendlines
A straight line drawn on a chart connecting successive swing lows in an uptrend or swing highs in a downtrend.
Triangle Patternalso: ascending triangle, descending triangle, symmetrical triangle
A chart pattern in which price swings narrow between two converging trend lines.
TVLalso: total value locked
Total value locked: the combined market value of all assets deposited in a DeFi protocol or on a blockchain.
Two-Factor Authenticationalso: 2FA, two-factor, two-step verification
A login method that requires a second proof of identity, such as a code from an app or a hardware key, in addition to a password.

U
