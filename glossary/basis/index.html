Glossary term

Basis

Also called: futures basis, futures premium

The difference between the price of a futures contract and the spot price of the same asset.

Basis is usually given as futures price minus spot price, in currency or as a percentage, sometimes annualised. It is positive in contango and negative in backwardation, and for dated futures it shrinks toward zero at expiry. Basis reflects financing costs and the balance of demand for leverage; it is not a forecast of the spot price.

ExampleHypothetical illustration

Hypothetical: with spot at 100 and a three-month future at 102, the basis is 2, or 2%.
