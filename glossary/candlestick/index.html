Glossary term

Candlestick

Also called: candle, candlestick chart, Japanese candlestick

A chart symbol that shows the open, high, low and close of one time period as a thick body with thin lines above and below.

The body spans the open and the close, and the wicks reach to the high and the low. Colour shows direction: commonly green or white when the close is above the open, and red or black when it is below. A single candle is a summary of past trading, and its meaning depends heavily on the candles and levels around it.
