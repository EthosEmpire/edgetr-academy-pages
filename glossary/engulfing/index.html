Glossary term

Engulfing Pattern

Also called: engulfing, engulfing candle, bullish engulfing, bearish engulfing

A two-candle pattern in which the second candle's body completely covers the body of the first, in the opposite direction.

A bullish engulfing is a down candle followed by a larger up candle after a decline; a bearish engulfing is the reverse after an advance. It is conventionally read as a possible shift in control between buyers and sellers. The pattern appears frequently and is often not followed by a reversal, so the surrounding context matters.
