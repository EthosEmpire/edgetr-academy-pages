Glossary term

Breakout

Also called: break out

A move in which price pushes above a resistance level or out of a chart pattern.

Breakouts are often judged by a close beyond the level, sometimes together with higher volume. Many fail: price returns inside the prior range, which is called a fakeout. Some writers use breakout for a move in either direction and keep breakdown for the downside only.

Learn more

Support & Resistance Breakouts A breakout is a move by price beyond a watched area, most often a close above a resistance zone or the edge of a range. It shows that the area gave way, not that the move continues. Beginner Trading Styles Breakout Trading Breakout trading is an approach built around price leaving a range, level or pattern, on the idea that a new move may follow. Its characteristic cost is the failed breakout. Intermediate
