Contact and support

How to reach the team behind edgeTR Academy, and what we can and cannot help with.

Email

info.ethosempire@gmail.com

edgeTR Academy is part of Ethos Empire. This address is the contact for the whole site. We read what arrives and aim to reply, but we cannot promise a reply time.

What we can help with

  • Corrections. If something on a page is wrong, unclear or out of date, tell us the page address and what you found. When we confirm a mistake, we correct it and update the page date.
  • Questions about how the site works. Where a lesson is, how search works, what a term in the glossary means.
  • Permissions. Using our material in a way the terms do not already allow.
  • Accessibility problems. If something is hard to read, reach or use, say what and on which device.

What we cannot help with

  • Personal financial, investment, tax or legal advice. The Academy teaches general concepts. It cannot tell you what to do with your money, review your trades or comment on a specific asset. See the educational disclaimer.
  • Wallet, exchange or account problems. We have no access to any wallet or account, and we cannot recover funds or reverse transactions.

Stay safe

We will never ask you for a seed phrase, private key, password or payment, and we never offer to “recover” lost funds. Anyone who contacts you in our name and does, is trying to scam you. Please do not send them to us in an email either.
